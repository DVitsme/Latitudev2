## Do You Want to Make an Impact? Join Our Team!

Encore Insurance Group is seeking an ambitious, self-driven, and people-oriented professional to join our growing team! If you are a strong communicator, take initiative, and have a passion for helping others while achieving financial independence, this could be the perfect opportunity for you.

### About Us
Encore Insurance Group is one of the rarest minority-owned insurance agencies in the state of Virginia. We embrace a start-up culture with a tight-knit, collaborative team and a mission-driven approach to serving our community.

Our flexible, remote-friendly environment allows for a work-life balance, and we provide an opportunity to make a meaningful impact by offering business and insurance solutions to commercial clients. Join a group of highly respected professionals who are passionate about growth, equity, and community engagement.

### Job Summary
As a member of our dynamic team, you will:

* Build and maintain strong relationships with clients, key decision-makers, and business partners.
* Host and participate in community events to enhance brand awareness and attract new clients.
* Identify and pursue new business opportunities while offering innovative, tailored solutions.
* Partner with clients to understand their goals and provide strategies that meet their needs.
* Develop and execute strategic business plans to expand the company's client base and market presence.
* Stay informed on industry trends, market shifts, and competitor products.
* Prepare and present reports on sales performance, forecasts, and expenses to management.
* Collect and analyze client feedback to improve service delivery and client satisfaction.
* Maintain organized and accurate records of activities and performance in the company CRM system.

### Requirements
* **Education:** High School Diploma or GED required.
* **Experience:** Minimum 1 year of work experience (sales experience preferred). No prior insurance experience necessary—full training provided.
* **Tech:** Proficiency with information systems (Microsoft Office, AgencyZoom, AMS 360).
* **Skills:** Excellent communication, negotiation, and presentation skills. Strong interpersonal and leadership abilities.
* **Traits:** Highly organized, capable of working independently, motivated, goal-oriented, and professional attitude.
* **Background:** Ability to pass a background check.
