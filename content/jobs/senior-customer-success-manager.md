## Are You Obsessed with Client Growth? Join Our Rocket Ship!

CloudPeak Solutions is looking for a strategic, empathetic, and data-driven Customer Success Manager to own our enterprise portfolio. If you thrive on solving complex problems, building deep relationships, and turning customers into raving fans, we want to meet you.

### About Us
CloudPeak Solutions is a Series B funded SaaS platform revolutionizing the logistics and supply chain industry. We help companies move goods faster, cheaper, and more sustainably.

We are a team of builders, dreamers, and doers who believe that "Customer Obsession" isn't just a value on a wall—it's how we operate every single day. We offer a fast-paced, high-trust environment where your voice matters and your impact is visible from Day One.

---

### Job Summary
As the voice of the customer within our organization, you will:

* Manage a portfolio of 30-40 high-value enterprise accounts, serving as their primary strategic advisor.
* Lead the onboarding process for new clients, ensuring rapid adoption and "time-to-value."
* Conduct Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs) to showcase ROI, identify pain points, and align on future goals.
* Proactively monitor account health metrics (usage, NPS, support tickets) to predict and prevent churn.
* Partner with the Sales team to identify upsell and cross-sell opportunities within your book of business.
* Collaborate with Product and Engineering teams to advocate for feature requests and improvements based on client feedback.
* Develop and maintain customer success playbooks and best practices to share with the wider team.

### Requirements
* **Education:** Bachelor’s degree in Business, Communications, or related field preferred.
* **Experience:** Minimum 3 years of experience in Customer Success or Account Management, specifically within B2B SaaS.
* **Tech:** Proficiency with CRM tools (Salesforce, HubSpot) and CS platforms (Gainsight, ChurnZero).
* **Skills:** Exceptional presentation skills—you can command a Zoom room or a boardroom. Strong data analysis capabilities (Excel/Sheets wizardry is a plus).
* **Traits:** Empathetic listener, relentless problem solver, commercially minded, and resilient under pressure.
* **Background:** Must be authorized to work in the US without sponsorship.
