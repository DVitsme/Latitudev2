## Are You a Data-Driven Storyteller? Let's Create Magic.

PixelPerfect Strategies is seeking a creative and analytical Digital Marketing Specialist to manage our paid media campaigns. If you love digging into Google Analytics as much as you love crafting the perfect ad copy, you belong here.

### About Us
PixelPerfect Strategies is a boutique digital agency serving sustainable fashion and lifestyle brands. We don't just run ads; we build movements.

We are a small, agile team that values experimentation over perfection. We believe that the best marketing comes from a place of authenticity, and we are looking for a team member who isn't afraid to test bold ideas and pivot quickly based on data.

---

### Job Summary
As our primary Paid Media lead, you will:

* Plan, execute, and optimize paid campaigns across Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google Ads, and TikTok.
* Manage a monthly ad spend budget of $50k-$100k across multiple client accounts.
* Conduct A/B testing on ad creatives, audiences, and landing pages to improve ROAS (Return on Ad Spend).
* Collaborate with our design team to request high-converting visuals and video assets.
* Set up conversion tracking and pixels using Google Tag Manager.
* Generate weekly performance reports for clients, translating complex data into actionable insights.
* Stay ahead of the curve on platform updates, algorithm changes, and emerging digital trends.

### Requirements
* **Education:** Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Communications, or equivalent portfolio experience.
* **Experience:** 2+ years of hands-on experience managing paid social or search campaigns.
* **Tech:** Deep proficiency with Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Meta Ads Manager, and Google Ads.
* **Skills:** Strong copywriting skills—you know how to write hooks that stop the scroll. Mathematical aptitude for budgeting and forecasting.
* **Traits:** Curious, detail-oriented, self-starter, and legally eligible to work in the US.
* **Bonus:** Experience with email marketing platforms (Klaviyo) or SEO basics is a huge plus.
