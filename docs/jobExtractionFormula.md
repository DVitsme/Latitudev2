The Extraction Formula
1. Top Metadata (Location & Structure)
Source Fields: "Location" and "Position Type" (or "Schedule").

Formula: [City/State] + ( + [Remote/Hybrid Status] + ) + • + [Pay Structure]

Goal: Immediate logistical filtering. Candidates first look for where it is and how they get paid.


Example: "Virginia (Remote) • Commission-Only" 
+1

2. Job Title
Source Fields: "Job Title" or Header.

Formula: Direct copy. Keep it standard (avoid internal codes like "Producer II").


Example: "Commercial Lines Producer" 

3. Summary (The "Hook")

Source Fields: "About Us" , "Position Highlights" , and "Why Join?".
+2

Formula: [Who We Are/Culture] + [The Ideal Persona] + [The #1 Barrier to Entry Removed].

Logic:


Identity: "Join Encore Insurance Group, a minority-owned agency with a startup culture." 


Persona: "We are seeking ambitious individuals..." 


Barrier Removed: "No prior insurance experience is required—we provide full training..." 
+2

Constraint: Keep under 280 characters to prevent card overflow.

4. Badges (The "Perks")

Source Fields: "Why Join?" , "Requirements" , and "Highlights".
+3

Formula: Extract 4 key phrases (maximum 2-3 words each) focusing on Flexibility, Money, and Growth.

Priority Hierarchy:


Work Mode: (e.g., "Remote / Flexible" )
+2


Earning Potential: (e.g., "Unlimited Income" )
+1


Support: (e.g., "Training Provided" )
+2


Long-term Value: (e.g., "Equity Opportunity" )
+1

Applied Example
Raw Text Input:

"Position Type: Commission-Only... Location: Virginia (Remote Work Options)... No Experience Necessary (Training Provided)... Equity opportunities for top performers... Unlimited commission-based earning potential." 
+1

Extracted Output for Component:

Metadata: Virginia (Remote) • Commission-Only

Badges: ["Remote / Flexible", "Unlimited Income", "Training Provided", "Equity Opportunity"]
